Research notes: what the games have shown so far

Every human move in the games received is scored by the strongest AI, the same way as the hints on the play page. A move that loses 3 or more points by the AI's estimate counts as a mistake, and the notes below show where points tend to slip away. They are recounted automatically whenever a new game arrives. The AI's estimates are not exact either, so treat the points for a single move as a rough guide.

        How your games are used

        People play differently from the AI, so their games show it positions it has not learned yet. The positions from every game received are mixed into each round of self-play training. Moves after which the AI's own estimate dropped sharply (moves it had not seen coming) are collected in the weakness report (GitHub, Japanese) and used as clues to improve it.

        Sending a game, and replaying your own

        When you finish a game on the play page, the result panel shows a button to send the game record (no account needed). Records are checked against the rules and added to this list every morning. Only the deck, the moves, the scores and the AI level are sent; no names.

        Sending is entirely up to you. Without sending anything, you can still play and replay your own games as before.

        You can replay your own games without sending them, from "Replay a game" on the play page (this browser keeps your last 20 games). Use "Copy replay link" to show a game to a friend.
