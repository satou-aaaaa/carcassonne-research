Your first Carcassonne

Learn Carcassonne, the board game where you build a map out of tiles, step by step from zero. There are also tips and practice problems based on games played by the strong AI built in this project.

  1. RulesHow a turn works, placing tiles and meeples, and scoring, explained with diagrams.
  2. StrategyNext stop once you know the rules: thinking in point differences, the tile mix, hard-to-fill holes, plans for each stage of the game, common mistakes and a glossary.
  3. Lessons from the AIEasy-to-copy habits found by analysing games between strong AIs.
  4. Next-Move QuizFrom beginner to advanced. Guess where the AI would place, then read the explanation.
  5. Carcassonne by the NumbersHow big is the first-player advantage? Where do the points come from? Data from games between strong AIs, compared with chess, Go and other games.
  6. Community GamesReplay games other players played against the AI, move by move on the board.
  7. Play the AIPlay one-on-one against this project's strongest AI. Beginner mode also shows how many points each of your moves lost.

Suggested path: learn to play with the Rules page, read "Five things to learn first" in the Strategy guide, solve the beginner quiz problems, then play the AI in beginner mode and review your costly moves. Once you're comfortable, move on to Lessons from the AI and the advanced quiz problems.

Playing the AI needs no installation; the AI thinks inside your browser (the strongest setting can take a few seconds per move). The rules are the current rules of the base game (72 tiles).
