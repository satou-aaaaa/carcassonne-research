Your first Carcassonne

Learn Carcassonne, the board game where you build a map out of tiles, step by step from zero. You can play the strong AI built in this project, and learn from tips and practice problems based on its games.

Play the AIStart the course at chapter 1

Three parts

  • CourseFour chapters: rules, strategy, lessons from the AI, and a next-move quiz. For complete beginners and for players who want to improve.Course contents
  • ResearchWhat we learned from AI-vs-AI and human-vs-AI games: the first-player edge, where points come from, and positions people often get wrong.Research contents
  • Play the AIPlay one-on-one against the strongest AI. Nothing to install. Beginner mode shows how many points each of your moves lost.Start a game

Suggested path: learn how to play in chapter 1 (Rules), read "Five things to learn first" in chapter 2 (Strategy), solve the beginner set of the chapter 4 quiz, then play the AI in beginner mode and review the moves that cost you points.

The AI thinks inside your browser (the strongest setting can take a few seconds per move). The rules are the current rules of the base game (72 tiles). "Carcassonne" is a trademark of Hans im Glück; this is an unofficial fan site.
