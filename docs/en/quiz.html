Carcassonne Next-Move Quiz

Practice problems: guess the move a strong AI chose. Base game, two players.
The AI's evaluation is the final point difference (you − opponent) if you choose this move, as estimated by this project's Monte Carlo tree search with an evaluation function (about 60,000 simulations per position). It assumes the order of the remaining tiles is unknown, so real games may not go as the AI predicts. Treat a gap of a few points as “both are fine”. The answers and point gaps were rechecked with the same latest AI as the play page (gen912, adopted 2026-11-03). We recheck every time the AI gets stronger, and problems whose answer changed are removed until reviewed.
