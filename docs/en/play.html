Rules used by this engine (editions differ)
  • Base game, 72 tiles, two players. 7 meeples each.
  • Completed: city = tiles × 2 + shields × 2, road = tiles, monastery = 9.
  • End of game: unfinished cities and roads score 1 per tile (city shields 1 each), monasteries 1 + filled neighbours, farmers 3 per adjacent completed city.
  • The side with the most meeples scores (ties: both score in full).
  • Meeples on the board: standing ones are on cities, roads and monasteries; lying ones are farmers on fields (as in the real game). The Areas button shades every claimed feature in its owner's colour (yellow when tied).
  • A tile that fits nowhere is discarded and redrawn (same player's turn).
  • Controls: tap a green square to place the tile → rotate with ⟳ just below the tile or by tapping the tile again (it cycles through legal rotations only) → pick a meeple spot with a circle or button → ✓ Confirm (tapping the chosen circle again also confirms). ✕ cancels the placement.
  • “Hint” previews how the AI would place. “Undo” takes back your last move (the local version records how often, and those games are left out of strength statistics).
  • Hover over an empty square (tap on a phone) to see how many tiles left in the bag fit it.
  • Drag to move the board; zoom with the wheel (on a phone, pinch with two fingers, and move them to pan at the same time) or the +/− buttons. Hover over a placed meeple (tap on a phone) to see its feature and points.
