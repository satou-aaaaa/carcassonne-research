Researching Carcassonne

Findings from games between the strong AIs built in this project, and from games people played against the AI on the play page.

  • By the NumbersHow big is the first-player edge? Where do points come from? Data from strong AI-vs-AI games, compared with chess, Go and other games.
  • Community GamesReplay games other people played against the AI, move by move on the board.
  • Research notesPositions people often get wrong and common mistakes, from human-vs-AI games.

How the research is done, including the ideas that did not work, is recorded in the GitHub repository.
